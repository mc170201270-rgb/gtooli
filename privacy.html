Privacy Policy

This policy explains, in general terms, how information may be handled when you visit GTooli. GTooli is a static website hosted using GitHub Pages. Please review this policy whenever the website adds new services or features.

Information you enter into tools

Many tools are designed to process the text, numbers or files you provide directly in your browser. For those tools, the information is processed locally and is not intentionally sent to a GTooli-operated server. Avoid entering confidential, highly sensitive or personally identifying information into any online tool unless you understand how it works.

Hosting and technical information

When you visit a website, its hosting provider may process technical information such as IP address, browser type, requested pages, and the date and time of a request for security, delivery and operational purposes. GitHub's own privacy terms apply to its handling of data as the hosting provider.

Read GitHub's Privacy Statement.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

GTooli may add analytics or advertising services in the future. If such services are added, those providers may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information about visits or interactions according to their own policies. This policy should be updated to identify the actual services before they are enabled. Do not assume that an analytics or advertising service is active unless it has actually been installed.

External links

GTooli may link to third-party websites. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review the privacy policies of those websites before providing them with information.

Children's privacy

GTooli is a general-purpose tools website and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been submitted to us, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the website changes. The date at the top indicates when this page was last prepared or revised.

Contact

For privacy questions, please use the GTooli contact page.
