ABOUT US

About GTooli

GTooli is a collection of free, simple online tools designed to help with everyday tasks. It brings together calculators, text utilities, converters, image tools and creator helpers in one convenient place.

Our goal

Our goal is to make useful digital tools easy to access, straightforward to use and available without unnecessary complexity. We aim to keep the experience clear on both desktop computers and mobile phones.

How the tools work

Many GTooli tools process the information you enter directly in your browser. Where a tool uses browser-side processing, the information you enter is not sent to a GTooli server by that tool. However, the website host and any third-party services used on the site may process standard technical information as described in our Privacy Policy.

Accuracy and responsibility

Tools are provided for general information and convenience. Please check important results independently. Health-related calculators provide estimates only and are not a substitute for professional medical advice.
